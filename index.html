NetLemmings Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

NetLemmings ("the app") shows your phone's network activity as a scene of animated pixel characters on a home-screen widget, with charts of how much data each app has used. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything stays on your phone. The app collects nothing, shares nothing, and has no way to send anything over the internet.

What the app reads on your phone

To draw the widget and charts, the app reads, on your device only:

  • how many bytes each app has sent and received over Wi-Fi and mobile data (from Android's usage statistics);
  • the names and icons of your installed apps, so each character can be labeled and colored.

This information is used only to draw the widget, the charts and the optional upload alerts. It is never transmitted anywhere. It is not the content of your traffic: the app cannot see what you browse, send or receive, which websites or servers you connect to, or any messages or files.

What the app stores

The app saves only your own settings on your phone: number of characters per lane, walking speed, whether to hide system apps, whether alerts are on, the alert threshold, and your list of muted apps. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
Usage accessLets the app read per-app network totals from Android. You turn this on yourself in system settings and can turn it off at any time.
Foreground serviceKeeps the widget animation and the background upload check running. A small notification shows while it runs.
NotificationsShows the running notification and optional alerts when an app uploads a lot of data while your screen is off.
Network stateLets the app notice when the phone has no network connection, so the lemmings can react. It reads only whether a connection exists, not what is sent over it.
Receive boot completedRestarts the widget after your phone restarts.

The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code, and it has no accounts.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone, because we never receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to collect from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
